# 🗺️ Peru Community 311

**Peru Community 311** is an open-source, citizen-built civic reporting portal for Peru, Indiana. It provides a simple, map-based interface for residents to map non-emergency issues—such as potholes, stray animals, code violations, water leaks, and damaged park equipment—and automatically routes formatted reports directly to the responsible municipal departments.

> **Disclaimer:** *Peru Community 311 is an independent community initiative created by local residents to streamline constituent communications. It is not owned, operated, or managed by the City of Peru or Miami County government offices.*

---

## 🌟 How It Works

1. **Pinpoint Location:** Drop a pin on the interactive Leaflet map (centered on Peru, IN) or use your device's GPS.
2. **Select Issue Category:** Choose from local municipal categories configured in `templates.json` (Street Department, Animal Control, Building & Zoning, Utilities, Parks).
3. **Answer Quick Questions:** Complete a short questionnaire to provide critical details (traffic impact, hazards, species, urgency).
4. **Multi-Channel Dispatch:** 
   - **One-Click Email (`mailto:`):** Pre-fills a structured email in your native email client to send directly from your personal account.
   - **Copy to Clipboard:** Fallback button to copy formatted text for webmail or forms.
   - **Direct Phone Calls:** One-touch `tel:` triggers for high-urgency reports (like active water main breaks).
   - **Webforms & Social Media:** Direct links to official municipal portals and Facebook pages when email is unavailable.
